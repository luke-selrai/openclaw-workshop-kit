# Use - Day 1 With Your Assistant

You finished setup. Your assistant is ready. This folder gets you from "it's running" to "it's actually useful for my business" in your first sitting.

## What you will do here

1. **Run your first 5 prompts** - see [first-5-prompts.md](first-5-prompts.md). Copy-paste prompts with blanks to fill in. Covers writing a cold email, researching a competitor, drafting a sales page, and more. Built to eliminate the blank-page problem.
2. **Finish the workshop cleanly** - see [completion-guide.md](completion-guide.md). What to do in the last 10 minutes, how to test that everything works, and the month 2+ roadmap.
3. **Look up any term that's unfamiliar** - see [glossary.md](glossary.md). Plain-English definitions for anything Claude might mention (Code, MCP, skill, connector, plugin, Bun, and so on).

## What comes after

Once you are comfortable with day-1 prompts, move on to [skills/](../skills/README.md) to see everything your assistant can do for your business, grouped by problem it solves.

When something stops working, [troubleshoot.md](../troubleshoot.md) covers the common fixes.
