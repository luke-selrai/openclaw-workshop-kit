# Connectors — Plug Your Tools Into Claude

This folder has per-integration setup guides for the outside services your assistant can plug into — Google Workspace, Outlook, Xero, Stripe, Shopify, Slack, and more.

## You probably do not need to read these

For most business owners, the right move is to **just ask your assistant**:

> "Connect Google Workspace." → Claude runs the connector, handles the sign-in popups, tells you when it's done.
> "Connect Outlook." → same pattern.
> "Connect Xero." → same.

The assistant already knows how every connector below works — the connector logic lives as a skill inside the kit. The guides in this folder are a backup reference for you to read if you want to understand what is happening under the hood, or if a connector runs into a snag your assistant cannot fix on its own.

## When reading a guide here IS useful

- You want to preview what permissions a connector will ask for before you grant them
- You are on a work laptop with unusual restrictions and want to read ahead
- A known issue applies to you — see [../../known-issues/](../../known-issues/) for those
