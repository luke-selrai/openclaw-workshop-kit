# How to Use Your AI Assistant — Day to Day

Your assistant is set up. Now what? This guide covers how to actually use it every day, what to say when you're stuck, and how to get better results over time.

---

## Starting Your Assistant

Every time you want to use your assistant:

1. Open **VS Code**
2. Open the `my-assistant` folder (File → Open Folder → my-assistant)
3. Open the terminal (`` Ctrl + ` ``)
4. Type `claude` and press Enter

Your assistant will greet you, check your memory, and ask what you need.

That's the only command you ever need to remember: **`claude`**

---

## How to Talk to It

Your assistant understands plain English. You don't need to phrase things in a special way. Just describe what you want like you would to a person.

**These all work:**
- *"Write a LinkedIn post about what I do"*
- *"What should I post on social media this week?"*
- *"I need to send a follow-up email to a client who hasn't replied in a week"*
- *"Research my competitors and tell me what they're doing well"*
- *"I'm stuck on what to focus on today — what do you think I should do?"*

**The golden rule:** More detail = better output.

| Instead of... | Try... |
|---|---|
| *"Write a post"* | *"Write a LinkedIn post about my new service for small café owners"* |
| *"Send an email"* | *"Draft a follow-up to Sarah who attended my workshop last Tuesday"* |
| *"Research this"* | *"Research social media trends in Australia for hospitality businesses in 2026"* |

## What Can It Actually Do?

Here are the most common things workshop attendees use it for:

### Content & Marketing
- Write LinkedIn posts, Instagram captions, Facebook posts
- Create a 4-week content calendar
- Write email newsletters
- Draft ad copy (Google, Meta, LinkedIn)
- Rewrite robotic AI-sounding text to sound human

### Research
- Research your competitors (including visiting their websites)
- Find what customers complain about in your industry
- Summarise a YouTube video for you
- Research a topic and give you a structured report

### Sales & Emails
- Write follow-up emails
- Draft cold outreach messages
- Build an email sequence for new leads
- Write proposals

### Strategy
- Help you brainstorm ideas
- Review your business plan and challenge your thinking
- Help you prioritise your week
- Map out which tasks you should automate

### Admin (with Google Workspace connected)
- Read and summarise your inbox
- Draft email replies
- Check your calendar and plan your day
- Create Google Docs, update Sheets

## Using Skills

Your assistant has 86 specialist skills. A skill is like switching your assistant from "general helper" to "expert" mode for a specific topic.

You don't need to know skill names. Just describe what you want and your assistant will pick the right skill automatically.

But if you want to activate a specific skill, say:

> *"Use the copywriting skill to rewrite this landing page copy"*
> *"Use the research skill to find competitors in [your industry]"*
> *"Use the email-sequence skill to build a welcome series for new customers"*

See the full list in [docs/SKILLS-REFERENCE.md](SKILLS-REFERENCE.md).

## Teaching Your Assistant Over Time

Your assistant remembers things. The more you tell it, the better it knows your business.

**After any session, you can say:**
- *"Remember that I prefer emails under 150 words"*
- *"Remember that my target customer is [description]"*
- *"Remember that I never post on Sundays"*
- *"Update my profile — I've started offering [new service]"*

It will save this in your memory file and apply it in every future conversation.

**Ask your assistant to read its memory:**
> *"Read my profile and tell me what you know about me and my business."*

This is a good way to check what it has remembered and fix anything that's wrong.

## When You're Not Happy With the Output

If the result isn't what you wanted, don't start over — just redirect:

| Problem | What to say |
|---|---|
| Too long | *"Shorten this to 100 words"* |
| Too formal | *"Make this more casual and conversational"* |
| Too vague | *"Be more specific about [topic]"* |
| Sounds like AI | *"Humanise this — remove the AI patterns"* |
| Wrong tone | *"Make this sound more like me — [describe your style]"* |
| Missed the point | *"That's not quite right. What I actually want is [explain again]"* |

Your assistant won't take offence. Just tell it exactly what you want.

## What to Do When You're Stuck

### "I don't know what to ask"

Say: *"Based on what you know about my business, what's the most valuable thing you could help me with today?"*

Your assistant knows your business. It will suggest where to start.

---

### "I'm overwhelmed and don't know where to focus"

Say: *"I'm feeling overwhelmed. Here's what's on my plate this week: [describe your situation]. Help me figure out what to prioritise and what I could hand to you."*

---

### "I want to try something but don't know if you can do it"

Just ask: *"Can you [describe what you want to do]?"*

If it can do it, it will. If not, it will tell you what's possible instead.

---

### "I want to do something I saw in the workshop but I can't remember how"

Say: *"During the workshop I saw you [describe what you saw]. Can you show me how to do that?"*

## Daily and Weekly Habits That Work Well

These are habits that workshop graduates say make the biggest difference:

**Morning:** *"Run my daily briefing — check emails, calendar, and tell me my priority for today."*

**During the day:** *"I need to [task]. Help me do this."* — use it for any task, big or small.

**Before client meetings:** *"Summarise everything I know about [client name] before my meeting with them."*

**End of week:** *"Give me a summary of what we did this week and what's still outstanding."*

**When writing anything:** *"Review this [email / post / proposal] and improve it. Keep my voice but make it sharper."*

## How to Keep Improving Results

**Give feedback:** After any output, say what you liked and didn't like. Your assistant learns from this.

**Be specific:** The more detail you give, the better the output. Industry, city, customer type, tone, length — all of it helps.

**Build on previous work:** Your assistant remembers the whole conversation. You can say *"take the email you just wrote and turn it into 3 social posts."*

**Ask for options:** Instead of one output, ask for three. Say *"give me 3 versions — one formal, one casual, one bold."*

**Use it for thinking:** You don't have to produce something. You can just talk through a problem. *"I'm trying to figure out [problem]. Help me think through it."*
