# Install, Before the Workshop

What to sign up for and what to install on your laptop **before** workshop day. If you do these two things in advance, you will be ready to go the moment the session starts.

## What you will do here

1. **Pick a subscription and install Claude Desktop**, see [subscriptions-and-software.md](subscriptions-and-software.md). One paid subscription ($100/month Claude Max), one free download.
2. **Create the free accounts you will need later**, see [accounts-and-logins.md](accounts-and-logins.md). Google Workspace, Anthropic, and a few optional ones depending on what you use.

## Time budget

Around 20-30 minutes total. Most of it is waiting for downloads. None of it needs a technical person.

## What comes after

Once you are signed up and installed, move on to [start/](../start/README.md), that is the walkthrough you run on workshop day to actually bring your assistant to life.
