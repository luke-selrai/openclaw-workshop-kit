Privacy Policy

For the QuickBooks Workshop Connector. Last updated: 2026-06-02.

1. Summary

This Connector runs entirely on your own computer. Your QuickBooks data is not transmitted to SelrAI's servers at any point. Below is a precise account of which third parties do see your data, and why.

2. Data flow

PartyWhat they seeWhy
YouEverythingThe Connector runs on your computer at your direction.
IntuitOAuth requests, API queries, and your own QuickBooks dataThe Connector calls Intuit's QuickBooks Online API to read and modify the data you ask it to act on. Governed by Intuit's privacy policy.
Anthropic (Claude)The prompts you write and the QuickBooks data the Connector returns to ClaudeClaude processes your conversation and decides which Connector tools to call. Governed by Anthropic's privacy policy and your Claude usage agreement.
CloudflareHTTPS requests to the page hosting this document (and the temporary OAuth tunnel during install)Cloudflare Pages hosts these legal pages on your own Cloudflare account. The OAuth tunnel forwards your browser's callback to your local machine during install only.
SelrAINothing about your QuickBooks dataSelrAI publishes the installer. The installer runs locally; SelrAI does not receive credentials, tokens, or QuickBooks records.

3. Credentials and tokens

Your Intuit Client ID, Client Secret, OAuth Refresh Token, and Realm ID are stored locally on your computer in ~/.local/share/qbo-mcp/.env (file mode 600, readable only by your user account) and in Claude Code's MCP configuration. They are never transmitted to SelrAI.

4. Logs

The MCP server logs HTTP request/response activity to standard output when you run it. These logs stay on your computer.

5. Your rights

You control your data. You can revoke the OAuth grant from your QuickBooks account settings at any time, and you can delete the Intuit app or the Connector files from your machine at any time.

6. Changes

If SelrAI updates the installer in a way that changes data flows, you will see the new privacy policy bundled with the updated installer. You can deploy the updated copy by re-running the installer.

7. Contact

For workshop-kit questions, contact the SelrAI team. For data-rights questions about QuickBooks data itself, contact Intuit.
